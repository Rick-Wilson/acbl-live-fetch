Bridge Classroom Fetch — demonstrations

Short recordings of the two paths that need a third-party login.

Most of this extension needs no account at all. Three of the four supported sources are public, including a real club game with a full field of results. Those can be exercised directly, in about a minute, and they demonstrate every part of the extension — injection, extraction, and hand-off. See the test procedure supplied with the submission.

The two paths below need a login, and we cannot supply credentials for them. Bridge Base Online permits only one active session per account, so a shared login would sign reviewers out of one another; and a newly created account has no played history to read, since these features read the results of sessions you have played. The ACBL login is a personal membership record. These recordings stand in their place.

Opponents' names are blurred or replaced throughout, for the same reason the extension does not collect them.

1. BBO — one session's results

The button merges into the hands-list table header. Clicking it reads every board of that session, across the field, and opens the analysis.

2. BBO — a range of past events

From the BBO lobby, a date range is chosen and each event fetched in turn, with a progress count and a cancel control throughout.

3. ACBL Live — a tournament

On live.acbl.org, the button appears beside the heading and extracts every section of the event, not only the one on screen.
